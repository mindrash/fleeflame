About Garden ROI

Get the App: Garden ROI is launching on iOS and Android.

Top Pages: Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions · Support


Garden ROI helps you answer one practical question: is my garden saving me money? The app turns home gardening into measurable results by tracking harvest value, expenses, and net savings in one simple workflow.


What Garden ROI Tracks

  • Harvest Value: Log what you harvest and estimate market value over time
  • Garden Expenses: Record seeds, soil, tools, water, and other costs
  • Break-Even Progress: See how close your garden is to paying for itself
  • Season Insights: Compare outcomes and learn what is worth planting next

Designed for Different Gardeners

Garden ROI supports different tracking styles so you can choose the level of effort that fits your routine:

  • Casual: Quick unit-based estimates for fast logging
  • Standard: Counts and categorized expense tracking
  • Hardcore: Precise weight-based tracking and deeper analytics

Local-First and Privacy-Aware

Core garden and financial data is stored locally on your device for an offline-first experience. For full details, review the app-specific Privacy Policy.


Free and Premium

Garden ROI includes a free tier for core tracking plus optional premium upgrades for expanded capabilities. Subscription billing is handled by the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
