Privacy Policy - Garden ROI

Effective Date: March 28, 2026


flee flame values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Garden ROI ("the App") handles your data when you use the app.


Information We Collect

Garden ROI is designed with a local-first architecture. Core garden and finance data is stored on your device using local storage.


Data Stored on Your Device

  • Garden details such as garden names, zones, and planting records
  • Harvest logs, estimated value calculations, and seasonal summaries
  • Expense entries such as seeds, soil, tools, water, and notes
  • App preferences including tracking tier and unit settings

This data is intended to stay local to your device for normal app usage.


No Account Required for Core Use

Garden ROI does not require account creation to use core tracking features.


How Data Is Used

Data is used to provide app functionality, including:

  • Calculating harvest value, net savings, and break-even progress
  • Showing analytics, reports, and planning insights
  • Restoring your settings and preferences on the same device

Subscriptions and Purchases

Garden ROI offers optional premium subscriptions. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and entitlement access.

  • RevenueCat: May process anonymous subscription identifiers and purchase state needed to activate premium features
  • App Stores: Handle billing and payment data directly; flee flame does not receive your full payment card details

Diagnostics and Reliability

To improve app stability, we may use crash/error monitoring tools that collect technical diagnostics (for example, app version, device model, OS version, and crash traces). These signals are used to fix bugs and improve reliability.


Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share limited data with service providers when needed to operate subscriptions and reliability services.


Data Retention and Deletion

Because core data is stored on your device, uninstalling the app generally removes local app data. You can also clear app data via your device settings where supported.


Children's Privacy

Garden ROI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.


International Users

The app may be available in multiple regions. Your use is subject to applicable local privacy laws.


Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.


Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted on our Support page. For company-level information, review the flee flame Privacy Policy.
