Is rEMDR a medical device?

No. rEMDR is for educational and self-help use and does not replace licensed mental health treatment.


Which tools are included?

Eye movement, audio bilateral, haptic bilateral, breath guide, body scan, and color flow sessions.


Does rEMDR have guided programs?

Yes. The app includes structured guided programs with multi-week progressions.


Where is my data stored?

Core session data is designed to remain local to your device whenever possible.


How do I get support?

Visit the Support page and include your device, app version, and a short issue summary.
