Bilateral Stimulation Tools

  • Eye Movement: visual tracking patterns with configurable speed and duration
  • Audio Bilateral: alternating left/right tones for headphone-based sessions
  • Haptic Bilateral: alternating vibration patterns for tactile pacing
  • Breath Guide: paced cycles such as box breathing and 4-7-8 style flows
  • Body Scan: guided body-awareness progression for grounding routines
  • Color Flow: smooth visual transitions for calming focus sessions

Guided Programs

rEMDR includes structured multi-week programs to support consistency over time, from short reset plans to longer resilience-oriented tracks.


Insights and Mood Journey

The app tracks sessions, completion trends, tool distribution, and optional mood check-ins to help users reflect on routines and progress.


Privacy-First Product Design

Core session data is designed to stay local whenever possible. Review Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
