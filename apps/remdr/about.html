About rEMDR

Get the App: Google Play | App Store (iOS: In Review)


rEMDR is a mobile bilateral stimulation app designed to support EMDR-related routines for educational and self-help use. It combines session tools, structured guided programs, and progress insights so users can build consistent regulation habits over time.


Why People Use rEMDR

  • Multiple Bilateral Modalities: Visual, audio, haptic, breath, body-awareness, and color-flow options
  • Structured Programs: Multi-week guided protocols with progressive session plans
  • Insights and Continuity: Session history, completion trends, and usage distribution across tools
  • Between-Session Support: Practical routines for users working alongside licensed professionals

Core Tools and Capabilities

  • Eye Movement: Configurable visual bilateral patterns for focused tracking sessions
  • Audio Bilateral: Alternating tone sessions designed for left-right auditory stimulation
  • Haptic Bilateral: Alternating vibration sessions for tactile bilateral engagement
  • Breath Guide: Patterned breathing flows such as box breathing and 4-7-8 style pacing
  • Body Scan: Guided body-awareness sequences for grounding and regulation
  • Color Flow: Smooth visual transition sessions for calm focus states

Guided Programs and Progression

rEMDR includes a catalog of guided programs ranging from short reset workflows to longer resilience-building tracks. Programs are structured by week and session, helping users follow a clear progression rather than starting from scratch every day.


Insights, Mood, and Session Tracking

The app records completed and early-ended sessions, tool usage, and trend-oriented insights. Optional mood check-ins after sessions help users observe changes over time and reflect on consistency, streaks, and milestone progress.

Free and Premium Access

The free experience includes core tools and limited guided access, while premium unlocks expanded program access, broader configuration options, and extended usage without free-tier limits.


Important Safety and Scope Note

rEMDR is not a medical device and does not replace licensed mental health care. If you are in crisis or experiencing severe symptoms, contact a qualified healthcare professional or emergency services immediately.
