rEMDR is a mobile bilateral stimulation app designed to support EMDR-related routines for educational and self-help use. It combines session tools, structured guided programs, and progress insights so users can build consistent regulation habits over time.


Why People Choose rEMDR

  • Total Sensory Control: Visual, audio, haptic, breath, and color-flow modalities
  • Structured Protocols: 10+ multi-week guided paths for resilience
  • Visualize Your Progress: Session history, mood trends, and usage insights
  • Therapist-Ready Support: Practical grounding routines to use between professional sessions

Core Utilities and Capabilities

  • Master Your Focus: Configurable visual bilateral patterns for eye-tracking
  • Find Your Calm: Alternating tones designed for immersive auditory stimulation
  • Stay Grounded: Rhythmic tactile vibration patterns for somatic engagement
  • Breathe & Center: Patterned flows including box breathing and 4-7-8 pacing
  • Release Tension: Guided body-awareness sequences for present-moment grounding
  • Visual Calm: Smooth color-flow transitions for focused relaxation

Guided Programs and Progression

rEMDR includes a catalog of guided programs ranging from short reset workflows to longer resilience-building tracks. Programs are structured by week and session, helping users follow a clear progression rather than starting from scratch every day.


Who It’s For

Self-Guided UsersBuild steady grounding routines with short sessions.
Therapy ClientsUse between-session practice as directed by a licensed professional.
Busy AdultsFollow simple guided tracks without setup friction.
Wellness LearnersExplore bilateral and breath tools for educational self-help use.

Insights, Mood, and Session Tracking

The app records completed and early-ended sessions, tool usage, and trend-oriented insights. Optional mood check-ins after sessions help users observe changes over time and reflect on consistency, streaks, and milestone progress.

Important Safety and Scope Note

rEMDR is not a medical device and does not replace licensed mental health care. If you are in crisis or experiencing severe symptoms, contact a qualified healthcare professional or emergency services immediately.
