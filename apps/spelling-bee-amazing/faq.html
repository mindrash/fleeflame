Spelling Bee Amazing FAQ

About | Features | Changelog


Does the app support multiple language learning modes?

Yes. Language personas include cross-language spelling modes and dedicated Japanese learning paths in Romaji and Kana (Katakana).


What are the main practice modes?

Spelling, Vocabulary, Competition, Daily Word, and persona-based Language Modes.


Can I use custom word lists?

Yes, custom imports are available with premium access.


How does progress tracking work?

The app tracks accuracy, streaks, and word mastery signals across sessions.


How do I get support?

Visit the Support page and include your app version and issue details.
