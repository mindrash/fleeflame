This guide compares the five key practice paths in Spelling Bee Amazing.


Spelling Mode vs Vocabulary Mode

Spelling Mode focuses on hearing and producing the exact spelling. Vocabulary Mode starts from meaning, helping learners connect definitions to words.


Competition Mode vs Daily Word

Competition Mode is best for speed and pressure readiness. Daily Word is best for routine and long-term consistency.


Language Modes

Language Modes unlock cross-language spelling workflows by persona, including Japanese Romaji and Kana (Katakana) learning paths.
