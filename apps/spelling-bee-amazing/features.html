Lifelong Learning: Language practice can start early and still stay valuable across adulthood and older age when it becomes a consistent habit.


Core Learning Modes

  • Spelling Mode: hear and spell with immediate correction feedback
  • Vocabulary Mode: definition-first prompts to build recall and meaning
  • Competition Mode: timed rounds for contest-style pressure practice
  • Daily Word: quick daily challenge to maintain consistent habits
  • Language Modes: persona-driven cross-language learning, including French, Spanish, Portuguese, Vietnamese, plus Japanese Romaji and Kana (Katakana)

Persona-Based Language Paths

Each persona unlocks native-language workflows and bridge modes for cross-language study. Examples include English-to-French, English-to-Spanish, English-to-Portuguese, and English-to-Vietnamese pathways.


Progress and Mastery

  • Accuracy tracking and streak visibility
  • Word mastery progression with repeated correct answers
  • Session summaries for performance review

Free vs Premium

Free users get practical daily learning. Premium unlocks unlimited practice, advanced levels, competition access, custom word imports, and expanded session history.
