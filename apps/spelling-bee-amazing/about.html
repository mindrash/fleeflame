Spelling Bee Amazing is a mobile spelling and vocabulary training app built for consistent daily practice. It helps learners improve spelling accuracy, retain challenging words, and prepare for school tests and spelling competitions with focused, repeatable sessions.


Why Learners Succeed

  • Build a Habit: 5-minute daily sessions that fit into any home or school routine
  • Master Every Word: Multiple learning paths for spelling, vocabulary, and competition
  • Track Your Mastery: Accuracy, streaks, and session-level performance visibility
  • Family-Focused Experience: Clear prompts and pronunciation for all ages

Core Practice Modes

  • Spelling Mastery: Hear and spell words with instant feedback and hints
  • Expand Your Mind: Definition-first prompts to strengthen meaning and recall
  • Win the Bee: Timed rounds for pressure training and contest readiness
  • Daily Streak: Habit-forming challenge designed for long-term consistency
  • Global Learning: Persona-driven paths for French, Spanish, Portuguese, Vietnamese, and Japanese

Multilingual Learning Support

The app supports multiple language personas and localized experiences, including English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, and Vietnamese. This helps multilingual families and learners practice in a more natural learning context.


Persona Language Modes

Language personas do more than translate the interface — they also change which spelling language modes are available.

  • English persona: Core English modes (Spelling, Vocabulary, Competition, Daily) plus French, Spanish, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Japanese Romaji, and Japanese Katakana modes
  • French persona: Core French modes plus English-for-French, Spanish, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Japanese Romaji, and Japanese Katakana modes
  • Spanish persona: Core Spanish modes plus English-for-Spanish, French, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Japanese Romaji, and Japanese Katakana modes
  • Portuguese persona: Core Portuguese modes plus English-for-Portuguese, French, Spanish, Vietnamese, Japanese Romaji, and Japanese Katakana modes
  • Vietnamese persona: Core Vietnamese modes plus English-for-Vietnamese, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese Romaji, and Japanese Katakana modes

Japanese learning support: learners can practice Japanese in both Romaji mode and Kana mode (Katakana), supporting two distinct study approaches.

This setup supports both traditional same-language spelling practice and cross-language learning paths inside one app.


Smart Learning and Progress Tracking

  • Spaced Repetition: Learners revisit words that need reinforcement
  • Mastery Tracking: Progress toward mastered words is tracked over repeated correct attempts
  • Session Insights: Accuracy, session summaries, and streak indicators support goal-based practice
  • Voice and Pronunciation: Device text-to-speech helps learners hear words clearly before spelling

Research-Informed Learning Position

Spelling and language practice are cognitively engaging activities, and reading/spelling research supports links between literacy learning and adaptive brain systems for word recognition and language processing. We summarize evidence and sources on our Research Evidence page, and we avoid medical or guaranteed-outcome claims.


Built for Lifelong Learning

Language learning can feel easier when started young, but meaningful progress is possible at every age with consistency and the right level of challenge. Spelling Bee Amazing is designed to support this full arc: foundational practice for children, skill-building and language expansion for adults, and cognitively engaging routines that can remain valuable in older adulthood.
