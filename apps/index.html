Spelling Bee Amazing

Spelling Bee Amazing is a spelling and vocabulary practice app for students and families. It includes persona-based language modes: each persona has native spelling workflows plus cross-language learning modes, including two Japanese learning paths — Romaji and Kana (Katakana) — alongside smart review, pronunciation support, and progress tracking.

rEMDR

rEMDR offers bilateral stimulation session tools and guided protocols inspired by EMDR practices for self-help and educational use. It supports visual, audio, haptic, breath, and body-oriented session styles. The iOS App Store release is currently in review.
